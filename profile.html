02Who we are

A partner that knows the local market.

SAA Group was founded in Abu Dhabi in 1993 by Saeed Abdulla Rashed Al Shamsi, a UAE national who is our Owner and Chairman. Since then we have grown into a trusted name in recruitment and manpower outsourcing.

We take pride in our commitment to professionalism, integrity and client satisfaction. Our experienced team works to meet the needs of each client, from government entities to private companies, with a strong focus on the UAE’s Oil & Gas sector.

Chairman’s words

03Mission and values

What drives us.

Our mission

To connect talent with opportunity, providing outstanding recruitment and manpower outsourcing solutions that empower businesses to thrive. Through personalised service and a commitment to excellence, we aim to be the premier partner in driving success for our clients.

Our vision

To be the local symbol of excellence in providing quality manpower to diverse industries within the UAE, deepen our collaboration with present stakeholders, capture new markets and seek sustainable quality and growth.

Our values
