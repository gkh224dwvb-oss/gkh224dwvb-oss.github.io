How we deliver

From plan to people.

Service Realization is one of the five process areas of our quality system. It takes every service through four steps, with continuous client and candidate liaison all the way through.

  • Service Realization · four steps
  • Client and candidate liaison · continuous
See the full quality system
  1. 01

    Step 1: Service Quality Planning

    We plan the quality of each service before work starts. Quality processes are our driving tools for delivering quality services.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Clients Focus

    We conform to each client’s service requirements, rules and regulations. We hold the work to high standards and keep it reliable.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Design & Development Planning

    We design the service around those requirements. Every plan stays traceable and consistent with them, and feasible to implement.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Recruitment Planning & Implementation

    We plan the recruitment and carry it out. We look for the highest calibre applicants, with proper qualifications, relevant experience and a commitment to continuous improvement.
