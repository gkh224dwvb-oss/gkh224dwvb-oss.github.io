A message from our Chairman

“Client satisfaction is at the heart of our decision-making.”

Thinking ahead.

We understand that the business cycle is constantly changing. That is why we analyse current markets and look ahead to the future. Our clients’ demands have taken on a new dimension, and recruitment requirements have entered a new sphere.

At SAA we are constantly seeking to improve, and to provide every client with services tailored to their individual needs. We always listen to their demands and respond to their requests.

One of our main strategic objectives is to place greater emphasis on the recruitment and allocation of national talents. Emiratisation means “national jobs for national talents”.

To keep up with the competition we need to look beyond, which is why establishing connections with international partners is another strategic goal. Through diversification and innovative ideas, we look to move into new industries.

We are a recruitment organisation that really knows the local market, always ready to go the extra mile to make sure clients get what they are looking for. Client satisfaction is at the heart of our decision-making.

We look forward to doing business with you.

Mr. Saeed Abdulla Rashed Al Shamsi Owner and Chairman · SAA Group of Establishments

A message from our CEO

“The right people in the right roles, with quality every time.”

Building on 1993.

Our Chairman founded SAA in Abu Dhabi in 1993 with a clear commitment: excellent recruitment services that meet the manpower demands of the local market. That commitment still guides every decision we make.

Today SAA is a UAE Government licensed recruitment agency. We supply more than 2,500 employees, mainly to the Oil & Gas sector, and the group brings together five companies, from technical services and maintenance to facilities management, trading and real estate.

My focus is simple: the right people in the right roles, with quality every time. We hold our work to our clients’ requirements, rules and regulations. We stay in close contact with clients and candidates throughout, and we keep our communication open and clear.

We will keep building on the priorities our Chairman set out. Emiratisation comes first, so that more UAE Nationals build careers in the energy sector. Alongside it come international partnerships and new industries through innovation.

Thank you for your trust in SAA. Our team will be at ADIPEC 2026 at ADNEC Centre Abu Dhabi, and we would be glad to meet you there.

Mr. Shamis Alshamsi Chief Executive Officer · SAA Group of Establishments
