Onshore · Offshore

Manpower supply for onshore and offshore projects.

We provide manpower for onshore and offshore oil and gas fields and facilities. Today SAA supplies more than 2,500 employees, mainly to the Oil & Gas sector in the UAE.

Discuss this at ADIPEC
Licensed agency

Recruitment services.

SAA is a UAE Government licensed recruitment agency, founded in Abu Dhabi in 1993. Our recruitment specialists tailor each service to the client’s requirements, and quality processes drive how we deliver.

Discuss this at ADIPEC
Across the group

Facilities and maintenance.

The group also covers construction, maintenance and real estate, through SAA Facilities Management Est., SAA Contracting & General Maintenance Est. and SAA Real Estate.

Discuss this at ADIPEC
